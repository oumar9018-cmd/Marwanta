# MARWANTA — Roadmap

What happens next, in the order the funding plan pays for it. Every step
names its gate: nothing gated moves until the gate passes.

## Now — funded by Tranche 1 ($3,500)

- Order the servers (4 validators + 1 heavy verification server) and
  publish the setup.
- Finish the unfinished verification: NEGATIVE-CONTROL @ MaxSteps 10 to
  completion on the heavy server, full result published.
- Re-run the family configs at MaxSteps 15+ (infeasible on the old
  workstation) and publish the results.

## Next — funded by Tranche 2 ($3,500)

- Re-run the crash and partition test families across the four separate
  validator servers (not one machine), with logs and evidence published.
- Complete the gap / partition breadth-first searches that were left
  incomplete for lack of compute.

## Then — funded by Tranche 3 ($3,000)

- Independent external review of the spec and the evidence, published.
- A written, costed next-step pack: what a prototype phase would need.
  The decision to start it stays with the founder — it is not made here.

## Gated — NOT in this funding, NOT promised

| Item | Gate |
|---|---|
| Protocol / architecture approval (v0.4 or any version) | Founder review and approval |
| Prototype beyond the §4 minimum local prototype | Founder decision on the Tranche 3 pack |
| Private testnet | Readiness Assessment §3.3 items + founder authorization |
| Public testnet, mainnet, token | Separate decisions, each on its own evidence |

No dates are promised for any gated item. The roadmap is a sequence of
checkable work, not a launch timeline.
